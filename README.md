## 📬 How Alicia Submits Homework Audio to Preply (3 Steps)

1. **Record Directly in Browser**:
   Open [`Homework/Week-01-Interactive-Homework.html`](./Homework/Week-01-Interactive-Homework.html) in your browser. Scroll to **Part 4 (Live Audio Recording Studio)**, click **"Start Recording"**, speak for ~60 seconds, and click **"Stop Recording"**. Listen to your pronunciation immediately using the player.
2. **Download File**:
   Click **"⬇️ Download Audio File"**. It saves to your computer as `Alicia_Week1_Capstone_Sprint.webm`.
3. **Send on Preply**:
   Log into [Preply Messages](https://preply.com/messages) and open the chat with **Lewis O.** Click the **Paperclip icon (📎)**, attach your downloaded `.webm` audio file, and hit send!

> *Tip: A "📋 Copy Note" button is built into the web page to instantly copy a friendly submission note to paste into chat!*

## 📅 Weekly Module Breakdown

| Week | Core Phonetic Focus | Lexical / Discourse Upgrade | Capstone Scenario |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Stress-timing vs Syllable-timing; The Schwa (`/ə/`); Catenation (`‿`) | *To be fair, Quite frankly, At the end of the day, A bit of a stretch* | Media Orchestration vs Public Complacency |
| **Week 2** | Alveolar stop elision (`/t, d/`); Consonant gemination; Clean `/s/` | *Touch and go, Cut corners, Take with a pinch of salt, Iron out* | Product Launch Quality vs Deadline Realities |
| **Week 3** | Strong vs. Weak forms (`was, for, from, can, of`); Metric foot compression | British diplomatic understatement (*Not quite what I had in mind*) | Solo High-Stakes Negotiation Pushback |
| **Week 4** | Intrusive Glides (`[j]`, `[w]`); Eliminating glottal stops; `/b/` vs `/v/` | *Spot on, In a nutshell, Off the cuff, Step up, Wind down* | First 30 Days Leadership Welcome Speech |
| **Week 5** | Non-rhoticity in RP; Linking `/r/`; Intrusive `/r/` | *Entrenched views, At loggerheads, Stir up, Play down, Zero in on* | Algorithmic Echo Chambers & Political Debate |
| **Week 6** | Sentence Stress & Tonic Syllable (Nucleus) Placement; Decoding Subtext | *Keep in the loop, On the same page, Touch base, Across the board* | Boardroom Pitch & CFO Objection Handling |
| **Week 7** | British Intonation Contours (Fall-Rise `[\/]`, Definitive Fall `[\]`) | *Bury the hatchet, Clear the air, Skate on thin ice, Smooth over* | Executive Mediation & Crisis De-escalation |
| **Week 8** | The 4 Ps (Power, Pause, Pace, Pitch) & Vocal Modulation | *Bear testimony to, Tip the balance, Set a precedent, Usher in* | Impromptu Executive Keynote & Press Defense |

4. Click **Save**. Within 1–2 minutes, the live interactive portal will be available at:
