## 📋 What's in Week 1:

1. **Part 1 &bull; Consolidation (Exercises 1.1 & 1.2)**: 3-second rapid-fire cognitive sprints with a built-in 30s timer, plus Spanish-to-British reframers.
2. **Part 2 &bull; Interactive Intonation Device**: Click words in key executive sentences to discover how moving the **nuclear tonic stress** alters the hidden subtext heard by British colleagues. Includes audio playback!
3. **Part 3 &bull; Pronunciation Clinic**: Master British stress-timing with the Metronome Desk-Tap drill, the "Lazy Sigh" technique for the Schwa (`/ə/`), and consonant catenation (`‿`).
4. **Part 4 &bull; British Lexicon**: 3 essential executive expressions with real-world business context.
5. **Part 5 &bull; In-Browser Audio Studio**: Record your 60-second spontaneous speech sprint directly in your browser, listen back, and download your `.webm` voice note.

## 📬 How to Send Your Audio Note to Lewis on Preply (3 Steps)

1. **Record**: Go to **Part 5** in the [Interactive Portal](https://log210788.github.io/Alicia/), click **"Start Recording"**, speak for ~60 seconds, and click **"Stop Recording"**.
2. **Download**: Click **"⬇️ Download Audio File (.webm)"** to save your voice note.
3. **Send in Preply Chat**: Open [Preply Messages](https://preply.com/messages) in your thread with **Lewis O.**, click the **Paperclip icon (📎)**, attach your downloaded `.webm` audio note, and hit send!

> *Tip: A one-click "Copy" button is included on the page to quickly copy a ready-made chat note!*
