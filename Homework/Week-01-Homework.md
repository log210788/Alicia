## Part 2: Self-Guided Pronunciation Masterclass (The Rest of Week 1)

### The Metronome Principle: "Drum Beat" vs. "Machine Gun"
- **Spanish** is *syllable-timed* (Machine Gun): every syllable takes equal time (*da-da-da-da*).
- **British English** is *stress-timed* (Drum Beat): only stressed content words hit the musical beat (**DUM** ... da-da ... **DUM**). All unstressed words in between are squashed and reduced to **Schwa (/ə/)**.

#### The Desk Tap Exercise (Try This Now):
Tap your desk with your knuckle at a steady speed: *Tap ... Tap ... Tap*. 
Do **NOT** slow down your hand! Say all three sentences in the exact same duration:
1. *[Tap!] **CATS** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] **EAT** &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] **FISH**.* (3 beats, 3 syllables)
2. *[Tap!] The **CATS** &nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] will **EAT** &nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] the **FISH**.* (3 beats, 5 syllables)
3. *[Tap!] The **CATS** might have &nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] **EAT**-en all the &nbsp;&nbsp; [Tap!] **FISH**.* (3 beats, 8 syllables)

### How to Produce the Schwa (/ə/)
- **The "Lazy Sigh"**: Relax your jaw so it hangs half-open. Do not round your lips (no "o"), do not smile (no "e"). Let your tongue lie limp in the bottom of your mouth. Make a quiet grunt: `/ə/`.
- **Key Words to Practice**:
  - `c-o-mmunity` ➔ `/kəˈmjuː.nə.ti/` (never round lips on the first syllable)
  - `s-u-ppose` ➔ `/səˈpəʊz/`
  - `o-bserve` ➔ `/əbˈzɜːv/`
  - `wom-a-n` ➔ `/ˈwʊm.ən/`
  - `Hampshire` ➔ `/ˈhæmp.ʃə/` (British place names ending in *-shire* are always `/ʃə/`)

---

### Consonant-to-Vowel Catenation (`‿`)
English hates acoustic dead ends. If a word ends in a consonant and the next begins with a vowel, glide across:
- `hold ‿ on` ➔ `/həʊl-dɒn/`
- `turned ‿ out` ➔ `/tɜː-naʊt/`
- `first ‿ of ‿ all` ➔ `/fɜː-stə-vɔːl/`
- `pick ‿ it ‿ up` ➔ `/pɪ-kɪ-tʌp/`
