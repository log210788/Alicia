### Task 2: "Anti-Translate" Discourse Starter Clinic (Written Solutions)

1. *"El jefe no nos dio tiempo suficiente, pero él tampoco tenía la información."*
   - **Required Starter**: **To be fair,...**
   - **Model British English**: *"To be fair, the boss didn't give us enough time, but to be fair to him, he didn't have all the information either."*

2. *"Sinceramente, creo que este informe no sirve para nada y está mal enfocado."*
   - **Required Starter**: **Quite frankly,...**
   - **Model British English**: *"Quite frankly, I think this report is completely unhelpful and misses the core issues entirely."*

3. *"Decir que tendremos todo el proyecto listo el viernes es imposible."*
   - **Required Starter**: **It's a bit of a stretch to...**
   - **Model British English**: *"It's a bit of a stretch to expect the entire project deliverable to be completed by Friday."*

4. *"Al final lo que importa es si el cliente está contento con los resultados."*
   - **Required Starter**: **At the end of the day,...**
   - **Model British English**: *"At the end of the day, all that truly matters is whether the client is satisfied with the results."*

## Part 2: Solutions for Self-Guided Pronunciation Masterclass

### Task 3: Phonetic & Connected Speech Marking (Written Solutions)

#### 1. Sentence 1
> *It turned ‿ out to be <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>n ‿ un<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>xp<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>ct<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>d gath<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>ring ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>f c<span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>mmun<span style="text-decoration: underline;">i</span>ty l<span style="text-decoration: underline;">ea</span>d<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>rs.*

- **Catenation (`‿`)**:
  - `turned ‿ out` ➔ `/tɜː-naʊt/`
  - `an ‿ unexpected` ➔ `/ə-nʌn.ɪkˈspek.tɪd/`
  - `gathering ‿ of` ➔ `/ˈɡæð.ər.ɪ-ŋəv/`
- **Schwa (`/ə/`)**:
  - *c<u>o</u>mmun<u>i</u>ty*: first vowel reduces to `/kəˈmjuː.nə.ti/`
  - *l<u>ea</u>d<u>e</u>rs*: terminal *-ers* reduces to `/ˈliː.dəz/`
  - *<u>o</u>f*: weak form `/əv/`

#### 2. Sentence 2
> *Hold ‿ on ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span> second, t<span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span> be fair he made ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>n ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>norm<span style="text-decoration: underline;">ou</span>s ‿ effort.*

- **Catenation (`‿`)**:
  - `Hold ‿ on ‿ a` ➔ `/həʊl-dɒ-nə/`
  - `made ‿ an ‿ enormous ‿ effort` ➔ `/meɪ-də-nɪ-nɔː-mə-sefət/`
- **Schwa (`/ə/`)**:
  - *t<u>o</u>*: `/tə/`
  - *<u>a</u>n*: `/ən/`
  - *<u>e</u>norm<u>ou</u>s*: `/ɪˈnɔː.məs/`
  - *eff<u>o</u>rt*: British RP reduces terminal unstressed *-ort* to `/ət/` (`/ˈef.ət/`)

#### 3. Sentence 3
> *<span style="text-decoration: underline;">A</span>t th<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span> end ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>f th<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span> day, <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>n ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>ffic<span style="text-decoration: underline;">ia</span>l ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>nnounc<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>m<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span>nt w<span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>s made ‿ in Hampsh<span style="text-decoration: underline;">i</span>re.*

- **Catenation (`‿`)**:
  - `end ‿ of` ➔ `/en-dəv/`
  - `an ‿ official ‿ announcement` ➔ `/ə-nə-fɪ-ʃə-lə-naʊns-mənt/`
  - `made ‿ in` ➔ `/meɪ-dɪn/`
- **Schwa (`/ə/`)**:
  - *Hampsh<u>i</u>re*: British county ending *-shire* is `/ʃə/` (`/ˈhæmp.ʃə/`)
  - *<u>a</u>nnounc<u>e</u>m<u>e</u>nt*: `/əˈnaʊns.mənt/`
  - *w<u>a</u>s*: weak form `/wəz/`

#### 4. Sentence 4
> *Quite frankly, it ‿ is ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span> bit ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>f ‿ <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span> stretch t<span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span> s<span style="text-decoration: underline;">u</span>ppose she will brush ‿ it ‿ off.*

- **Catenation (`‿`)**:
  - `it ‿ is ‿ a` ➔ `/ɪ-tɪ-zə/`
  - `bit ‿ of ‿ a` ➔ `/bɪ-tə-və/`
  - `brush ‿ it ‿ off` ➔ `/brʌ-ʃɪ-tɒf/`
- **Schwa (`/ə/`)**:
  - *s<u>u</u>ppose*: initial syllable reduces to `/səˈpəʊz/`
  - *t<u>o</u>*: `/tə/`

#### 5. Sentence 5
> *C<span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>n ‿ I pick ‿ it ‿ up fr<span style="text-decoration: underline;">o</span>m th<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span> wom<span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>n <span style="text-decoration: underline;">a</span>t th<span style="text-decoration: underline;">e</span> front desk?*

- **Catenation (`‿`)**:
  - `Can ‿ I` ➔ `/kæ-naɪ/` (or `/kə-naɪ/`)
  - `pick ‿ it ‿ up` ➔ `/pɪ-kɪ-tʌp/`
- **Schwa (`/ə/`)**:
  - *C<u>a</u>n*: `/kən/`
  - *fr<u>o</u>m*: `/frəm/`
  - *wom<u>a</u>n*: second syllable reduces to /ˈwʊm.ən/

## Part 4: Capstone Audio Sprint (Model Native Transcript)

> **Prompt Reference**: *"Think about a recent news story or social media trend that caught your attention. In your opinion, was it an authentic social movement, or was it orchestrated to distract the public? Give your honest thoughts using at least two target phrases from Week 1."*

**Model Native Speaker Response (60-second delivery)**:
> *"**Quite frankly**, when I first observed the viral boycott spreading across social media last month, it struck me as remarkably artificial. **To be fair**, there were genuine grassroots concerns at the very beginning, but as soon as major political figures jumped on the bandwagon, the whole narrative felt heavily orchestrated to divert public attention away from economic issues. **At the end of the day**, claiming that millions of people mobilized spontaneously overnight is **a bit of a stretch**. In reality, algorithmic amplification turned what was a modest gathering into an intense media circus, and once you look past the noise, very little meaningful reform actually **turned out** from it."*
