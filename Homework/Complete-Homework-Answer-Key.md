#### Week 6: Nuclear Sentence Stress & Subtext
- **Subtext Decoding**:
  - *"I never said **SHE** stole the client."* ➔ Subtext: Someone else stole the client, not her.
  - *"I never said she **STOLE** the client."* ➔ Subtext: She acquired the client legitimately or through standard business channels, not theft.
  - *"We expected **YOU** to lead the presentation."* ➔ Subtext: We are surprised/disappointed that someone else stepped in instead of you.
  - *"We **EXPECTED** you to lead the presentation."* ➔ Subtext: That was the original expectation or plan, but it wasn't fulfilled.
