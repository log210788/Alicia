### Task 4: The 6-Month Autonomous Maintenance Routine
*Save this personal routine for ongoing fluency maintenance:*
1. **The 15-Minute Daily British Podcast Shadowing**: Listen to BBC Radio 4 (*In Our Time*, *Desert Island Discs*, *The Bottom Line*) or *The Rest Is Politics*. Shadow 2 minutes of speech out loud, matching the speaker's rhythm, schwa reduction, and pitch contours.
2. **The 3-Second Cognitive Rule**: Whenever you speak in English at work, banish internal Spanish translation. If you don't know a specific word, circumlocute—describe it using 5 simple English words.
3. **Weekly 2-Minute Speech Memo**: Once a week, record yourself giving an unscripted 2-minute update on your week. Listen back and audit your 4 Ps, weak forms, and connected speech.
4. **Maintenance Sessions with Lewis**: Continue periodic maintenance sessions on Preply (e.g. bi-weekly or monthly) to audit progress and refine your delivery under live conversational pressure.
