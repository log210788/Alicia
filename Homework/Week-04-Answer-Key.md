### Task 1: Glide Identification & Marking (Written)

**Linguistic Rules for Hiatus Resolution**:
- **Intrusive `[j]` (Y-Glide)**: Inserted when the first word ends in a **front, spread vowel/diphthong** (`/iː/`, `/ɪ/`, `/eɪ/`, `/aɪ/`, `/ɔɪ/`) and the next word starts with a vowel.
- **Intrusive `[w]` (W-Glide)**: Inserted when the first word ends in a **back, rounded vowel/diphthong** (`/uː/`, `/ʊ/`, `/əʊ/`, `/aʊ/`) and the next word starts with a vowel.

---

#### 1. Sentence 1
> *May ‿ **[j]** ‿ I ask if you ‿ **[w]** ‿ agree with the proposal?*

- `May` ends in `/eɪ/` (front) ➔ **[j]** glide into *I*
- `you` ends in `/uː/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *agree*

---

#### 2. Sentence 2
> *She wants to know ‿ **[w]** ‿ if they can see ‿ **[j]** ‿ it today.*

- `know` ends in `/əʊ/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *if*
- `see` ends in `/iː/` (front) ➔ **[j]** glide into *it*

---

#### 3. Sentence 3
> *You ‿ **[w]** ‿ always speak off the cuff so ‿ **[w]** ‿ effortlessly.*

- `You` ends in `/uː/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *always*
- `so` ends in `/əʊ/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *effortlessly*

---

#### 4. Sentence 4
> *Try ‿ **[j]** ‿ again to do ‿ **[w]** ‿ it before you go ‿ **[w]** ‿ away.*

- `Try` ends in `/aɪ/` (front diphthong) ➔ **[j]** glide into *again*
- `do` ends in `/uː/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *it*
- `go` ends in `/əʊ/` (back rounded) ➔ **[w]** glide into *away*

---

#### 5. Sentence 5
> *In a nutshell, they ‿ **[j]** ‿ ought to step up and buy ‿ **[j]** ‿ eight more units.*

- `they` ends in `/eɪ/` (front) ➔ **[j]** glide into *ought*
- `buy` ends in `/aɪ/` (front diphthong) ➔ **[j]** glide into *eight*

### Task 2: Acoustic Minimal Pair & Glide Self-Recording (Evaluation Rubric)

- [x] **Clear /b/ vs. /v/ Distinction**:
  - For `/b/` (*ban, boat, best, curb*): Both lips touch firmly and release with a gentle burst of air.
  - For `/v/` (*van, vote, vest, curve*): Upper front teeth contact the lower inner lip with continuous vocal cord vibration. Spanish bilabial fricative `[β]` is completely eliminated.
- [x] **No Glottal Sticking**: Gliding seamlessly from *say [j] it* and *do [w] it* without an acoustic pause or glottal stop `[ʔ]`.

### Task 3: Professional Reflection Writing

**Prompt**: *Concise 5-to-7 sentence reflection on entering a new company using at least 4 target phrases.*

**Model Answer**:
```text
Title: Navigating My First 30 Days in a New Role

Stepping into a high-level corporate environment always requires a strategic balance of active listening and decisive initiative. In a nutshell, my priority during these first few weeks is to observe internal stakeholder dynamics and identify where cross-functional processes need refinement. Before our initial department review, I made sure to brush up on our quarterly performance metrics so that my feedback was spot on. While leading an unscripted discussion with executive leadership initially felt intimidating, being able to speak off the cuff with confidence proved to be an invaluable asset. Taking the initiative to step up on this transformation project has energized my team, though I am equally intentional about taking time in the evenings to wind down and recharge.
```

*Expressions Integrated*:
1. **In a nutshell**
2. **Brush up on**
3. **Spot on**
4. **Off the cuff**
5. **Step up**
6. **Wind down**

### Task 4: The 90-Second "Off the Cuff" Monologue (Model Transcript)

> **Prompt Reference**: *"Introduce yourself to your new team at an informal welcome meeting. Explain your background, what excites you most about your new role, and how you approach challenges. Keep speech smooth, eliminate pauses with fillers, and use at least two target phrases."*

**Model Native Speaker Response (90-second delivery)**:
> *"Good afternoon everyone, it's wonderful to finally meet all of you in person. **To be fair**, while joining a new leadership team in the middle of a major fiscal quarter can be quite intense, I'm genuinely **chuffed to bits** to be here. By way of background, I've spent the past decade leading commercial growth initiatives across European markets, with a particular focus on cross-border operations and strategic alignment. **In a nutshell**, what excites me most about joining this department is the sheer caliber of talent in this room and our opportunity to scale our digital offerings. When it comes to tackling operational roadblocks, I don't believe in rigid formulas; I prefer to **weigh up** the data quickly and then empower teams to **step up** and take decisive ownership. Over the next fortnight, I'm keen to **touch base** with each of you individually to hear your perspectives on what's working and where we have room for improvement. Thank you for such a warm welcome, and I look forward to working closely with all of you."*
