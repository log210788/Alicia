### Task 1: Nuclear Stress & Subtext Analysis (Written)

**Core Linguistic Principle**:
In English intonation, shifting the **tonic syllable (nuclear stress)** dramatically alters the pragmatic meaning, presuppositions, and underlying subtext of an utterance, even when the syntax and vocabulary remain completely identical.

---

#### 1. Sentence A vs. Sentence B

> **Sentence A**: *"I never said **SHE** stole the client."*
- **Tonic Syllable**: `SHE`
- **Implied Meaning & Subtext**: 
  - The speaker acknowledges that *someone* stole or improperly took the client, but specifically denies accusing *her*. The suspicion falls on another colleague or competitor (*"I said John stole the client, not her"*).

> **Sentence B**: *"I never said she **STOLE** the client."*
- **Tonic Syllable**: `STOLE`
- **Implied Meaning & Subtext**:
  - The speaker concedes that she acquired, onboarded, or won over the client, but rejects the allegation that she acted dishonestly or illicitly (*"She didn't steal them; they came to her voluntarily because of poor customer service elsewhere"*).

---

#### 2. Sentence C vs. Sentence D

> **Sentence C**: *"We expected **YOU** to lead the presentation."*
- **Tonic Syllable**: `YOU`
- **Implied Meaning & Subtext**:
  - The emphasis is on personal accountability and identity. The team or leadership is expressing surprise, disappointment, or confusion that someone else took the stage or that the intended person delegated their responsibility (*"You were chosen for your expertise; why did David step in instead?"*).

> **Sentence D**: *"We **EXPECTED** you to lead the presentation."*
- **Tonic Syllable**: `EXPECTED`
- **Implied Meaning & Subtext**:
  - The emphasis is on past expectation versus present reality. It highlights that the prior plan or strong assumption was broken, or that something unexpected went wrong (*"We anticipated that you would lead, but circumstances apparently changed or you failed to show up"*).

I propose that we **touch base** with the finance committee tomorrow morning so I can **flesh out** our modular ROI model.
